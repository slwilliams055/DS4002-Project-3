MODEL PERFORMANCE SUMMARY
The ResNet50 model achieved a final test accuracy
of 97.81%, correctly classifying 134 out of 137
testing images.

The model achieved 100% precision for REAL images,
meaning none of the FAKE images were incorrectly
classified as REAL.

Recall for REAL images was 95.45%, with 63 of 66
REAL images correctly identified and 3 incorrectly
classified as FAKE.

The REAL F1-score was 97.67%, indicating a strong
balance between precision and recall.

The ResNet50 model substantially outperformed the
majority-class baseline accuracy of 51.82%.

MODEL LIMITATIONS
Training accuracy reached 99.71%, while final
validation accuracy was only 50.83%.

This large difference suggests potential problems
with generalization to the validation dataset,
differences between data sources, or inconsistencies
in the evaluation pipeline.

Despite the high test accuracy, the substantial
validation-test discrepancy should be investigated
before drawing strong conclusions about performance
on unseen images.

The test dataset also contained only 137 images,
limiting the certainty of performance estimates.

CONCLUSION
The ResNet50 model demonstrated strong classification
performance on the held-out test dataset, particularly
in identifying FAKE images without incorrectly
classifying them as REAL.

However, the unusually low validation accuracy
indicates that further evaluation is necessary
to confirm the model's reliability and ability
to generalize across different image sources.
